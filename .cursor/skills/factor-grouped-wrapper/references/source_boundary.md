# Source Boundary

Allowed inputs:

- User-provided factor banks and market data that the user has the right to use.
- Public factor definitions, papers, documentation, and examples with appropriate attribution.
- Synthetic data generated by the included smoke fixture for mechanical validation.
- User-provided configuration files and prior run directories for resume.

Do not include or publish without explicit rights:

- Private, leaked, confidential, or paywalled dataset exports.
- API keys, credentials, tokens, `.env` files, or private service endpoints.
- Real research factor values, OOS predictions, backtest holdings, or long-lived run artifacts.
- Local absolute paths, workflow logs, or machine-specific environment details in committed examples.

Maintain research integrity:

- Keep OOS dates sealed until `frozen_selection.json` exists.
- Do not use OOS backtest results to choose factors, seeds, group sizes, model parameters, or `min_delta`.
- Do not present validation Pearson IC, validation Sharpe, or OOS backtests as guaranteed returns, production readiness, trading advice, or an investment recommendation.
