# Lookahead Audit Boundary

This skill orchestrates metadata, declared time splits, provider time semantics, and external leakage evidence. It does not claim that a multi-language source tree is leak-free when no language analyzer is available. Dependency and local findings are retained separately; a conflict remains unresolved until human review.

A high-risk finding may leave result artifacts available, but it prevents a `verified` reproducibility conclusion and must be marked `result_validity: compromised` when applicable.
