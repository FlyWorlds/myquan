# Portable Runtime Loader

This file is the portable entry point for Hermes, OpenClaw, and other Markdown-capable agent runtimes.

## Load

1. Read `../SKILL.md` in full.
2. Select the complete scan or the smallest relevant focused script.
3. Prefer the existing programs under `../scripts/` to duplicated implementations.
4. Preserve risk levels, data gaps, and method limitations in the final report.

## Safety

- Use PandaData only for quantitative conclusions.
- Read credentials only from documented environment variables.
- Do not expose credentials, tokens, or other sensitive information.
- Do not promise returns, provide personalized investment advice, issue position instructions, or generate automatic trades.
- Clearly separate observed facts from proxies and inference; never treat `UNKNOWN` as low risk.
- State that results are for research and education and are not officially certified by QuantSkills.

## Default Task

Scan the current A-share market across global macro conditions, margin financing, valuation, market trends, capital flows, sector rotation, stock-specific events, and technical indicators, then summarize the overall risk level, main triggers, data gaps, and limitations.
